# Career onboarding and résumé side panel

- `/onboarding.html` collects private company-type, industry, discipline and work-setup preferences independently of public profile creation. New accounts without a saved profile are routed here after sign-in. Existing members can edit interests from the directory or their own profile.
- `career.js` preserves invite and job destinations through onboarding. Google uses the existing production OAuth redirect. Choices made as a guest are held in the originating tab until they are saved.
- The directory can sort by interest matches. Unknown company data does not exclude a job. Industry labels are inferred from the saved company description; the panel discloses this.
- “Check my fit” uses saved profile history or résumé text pasted for that job. The displayed percentage is equal-weight coverage of recognized topic cues in the stored job summary, title, and highlights—not an ATS score, hiring probability or complete qualification evaluation. No score is shown with insufficient history or fewer than two recognized job cues. Original listing and checked date remain visible.
- Draft building preserves profile facts and can reorder accomplishment lines. Pasted résumés are preserved as the starting draft. Drafts are private, saved by user/job, and editable only after Edit; users can delete, copy or download them. Nothing changes the public profile.
- AI refinement is explicit and opt-in. The server authenticates the Supabase access token, reserves an atomic per-user quota (five/day, 30 seconds between attempts), and calls Vercel AI Gateway using `AI_GATEWAY_API_KEY` or deployment `VERCEL_OIDC_TOKEN`. Model: `anthropic/claude-haiku-4.5`, confirmed in Gateway's model catalog. No new client credentials or service-role keys.
- Only the current draft and stored job description are sent to the model. The original remains unchanged until the user accepts a suggestion; accepting still requires Save. Provider/quota/timeouts/truncation are handled without replacing the draft. Gateway must have valid authentication and credits; otherwise editing/saving/export remain available.
- `supabase/career.sql` is the repeatable database setup. Preferences and résumé drafts have owner-only RLS. Quota storage is private; a narrow invoker RPC wraps the authenticated private quota function. SQL fixtures are rolled back and never modify real accounts.

Verification: `node --test tests/career.test.cjs tests/career-session.test.cjs tests/resume-api.test.cjs tests/filter.test.cjs tests/recommendations.test.cjs tests/trueup.test.cjs`; execute `tests/career-access.sql` against Supabase. Browser checks cover guest onboarding choices/sign-in gate and desktop/mobile folder panel. Public profiles expose neither preferences nor drafts.
